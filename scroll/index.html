Scroll input

The canvas behind this page is fixed to the viewport and reads one number: how far down the page you are, from 0 at the top to 1 at the bottom. Scroll, and the selected sketch follows.

Speed, not just position

Flick the page. Every row of bars is knocked sideways in proportion to how fast you are scrolling and settles when you stop. Any sketch can read that as uScrollVelocity.

A block in the flow

The canvas below sits in the page like an image would. It measures its own trip through the viewport instead of the whole page: scattered as it enters, assembled when it is centred, scattered again as it leaves.
